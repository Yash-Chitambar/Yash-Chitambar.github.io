I'm an undergraduate at UC Berkeley studying Computer Science and Data Science, with a domain emphasis in Robotics. My research interest is making robots learn: dexterous manipulation, world models, and the infrastructure to evaluate and serve them.

I'm an undergraduate machine learning researcher at the [Berkeley AI Research Lab (Autolab)](https://autolab.berkeley.edu/). Previously, I was a software engineering intern at Salesforce (AI Infrastructure), where I built a semantic data layer for LLM agents and foundation-model-based forecasting services, and at Selector.ai, where I built real-time ML pipelines for time series summarization.

**Hobbies:** triathlon, product design and CAD, piano, mountain biking, and football.
